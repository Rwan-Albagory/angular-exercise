Our story

RUNAQ began at a single workbench with one idea: jewelry should be made slowly, by hand, and built to be worn for years, not seasons.

Shop the collection تسوّقي المجموعة

We started RUNAQ because most fine jewelry felt either mass-produced or out of reach. So we brought goldsmiths, stone-setters, and designers into one small atelier, and kept every step under one roof — from the first sketch to the final polish.

A decade later, the workbench has grown, but the promise hasn’t changed: certified gold, honest pricing, and a piece that’s checked by hand before it ever reaches you.
